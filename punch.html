Hawaiian Punch(Fruit Juicy Red)

The classic refresher of American children since, like, the '70s, Hawaiian Punch was made with real fruit juice back then, and the current label has pictures of fruit on the cover. In Japan, they let you put fruit on the label if the drink's got real fruit in it, so maybe that'll apply here? Plus, that red color is increasingly hypnotizing the more you look at the bottle.
